A few things that come up, and how to reach me.

How does the silent wake work?

Snoozzo is a watch-only alarm. At the time you set, it vibrates on your wrist. There is no sound — no speaker, no ringtone. If you turn on the pre-alarm, a soft crescendo starts a few minutes earlier. The person next to you can keep sleeping.

Do I need to wear the Watch, and keep it charged?

Yes. You only feel the alarm if the Watch is on your wrist and has enough battery to last the night. Charge it before bed, and wear it when you sleep. If it’s on the charger or the nightstand, you won’t feel a thing — and Snoozzo can’t confirm you got up either.

What is the wake check?

When you tap I’m up, Snoozzo asks you to tap three lights in the order they lit up, so it knows you’re actually awake. About three minutes later it asks again (“Still awake?”). If you miss either check, the buzzing comes back.

Does it work without the iPhone nearby?

Snoozzo runs on the Watch — there’s no iPhone app to keep open. Open Snoozzo on your Watch within 36 hours of the alarm so it can arm. Once it’s armed, the Watch handles the wake itself.

Can I change how the buzzing feels?

Yes. You can set intensity to Soft, Medium, or Strong, and choose a Pulse, Wave, or Double pattern. You can also set a pre-alarm ramp and limit how many times you’re allowed to snooze, or turn snooze off.

Does Snoozzo collect my data?

No. There’s no account, and the app doesn’t use the network. Alarms, settings, and streaks stay on the Watch. The details are in the privacy policy.

How do I get in touch?

Email support@snoozzo.com and I’ll get back to you. Please include your Apple Watch model and watchOS version if something isn’t working as expected.
