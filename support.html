Questions about getting up quietly? You’re in the right place.

How does the silent wake work?

Snoozzo is a watch-only alarm. At the time you set, it vibrates on your wrist. There is no sound — no speaker, no ringtone. The person next to you can keep sleeping.

Do I need to wear the watch, and keep it charged?

Yes. You only feel the alarm if the watch is on your wrist and has enough battery to last the night. Charge it before bed, and wear it when you sleep. If it’s on the charger or the nightstand, you won’t feel a thing — and Snoozzo can’t confirm you got up either.

What is the wake check?

When the alarm goes off, Snoozzo asks you to complete a short check on the watch, so it knows you’re actually awake and not just dismissing it in your sleep. A few minutes later, it checks again. If you miss either one, the buzzing comes back.

Can I change how the buzzing feels?

Yes. You can set haptic intensity and pattern, add a gentle pre-alarm that builds up before the main wake, and limit how many times you’re allowed to snooze. Those settings live on the watch.

Is there an iPhone app?

No. Snoozzo lives on the Apple Watch. You set alarms, change settings, and see your wake streak there.

Does Snoozzo collect my data?

No. There’s no account, and the app doesn’t use the network. Alarms, settings, and streaks stay on the watch. The details are in the privacy policy.

How do I get in touch?

Email support@snoozzo.com and I’ll get back to you. Please include your Apple Watch model and watchOS version if something isn’t working as expected.
