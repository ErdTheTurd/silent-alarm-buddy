Wake up without waking the person next to you.

Snoozzo is a watch-only alarm for Apple Watch. It vibrates on your wrist. It doesn’t make a sound.

Coming soon to the App Store

Most alarms wake the whole room.

A phone on the nightstand doesn’t care who’s trying to sleep. Snoozzo stays on your Watch. At the time you set, you get a repeating buzz — no speaker, no ringtone, no song through the wall. Your partner, roommate, or sibling can keep sleeping.

How it works

  1. Set the time on your Watch. There’s no iPhone app to keep open. Open Snoozzo on the Watch within 36 hours of the alarm so it can arm.
  2. It buzzes. That’s it. If you turn on the pre-alarm, a soft crescendo starts a few minutes early. At the set time, the Watch keeps a repeating buzz going until you tap I’m up. You can cap how many snoozes you get, or turn snooze off.
  3. Then it checks that you’re actually up. It asks you to tap three lights in the order they lit up — so you can’t just dismiss it and roll over. About three minutes later it asks again. Miss either check, and the buzzing comes back.

Your morning, on your Watch.

You can set the buzz to Soft, Medium, or Strong, and pick a Pulse, Wave, or Double pattern. A wake streak counts only the mornings you finish both checks. Alarms, settings, and that history stay on the Watch.

Nothing leaves your Watch.

Snoozzo doesn’t create an account and doesn’t use the network. Your alarms, feel settings, and wake history are stored on the device. There’s nothing to sign into, and nothing is sent to Edward Dunn or anyone else. The details are in the privacy policy.

A few questions

Does it work without the iPhone nearby?

Snoozzo runs on the Watch — there’s no iPhone app to keep open. Open Snoozzo on your Watch within 36 hours of the alarm so it can arm. Once it’s armed, the Watch handles the wake itself.

What if my Watch dies overnight?

You only feel the alarm if the Watch is on your wrist and has enough battery to last the night. Charge it before bed. If it’s on the charger or the nightstand, you won’t feel a thing.

Does it make any sound?

No. There’s no speaker and no ringtone. Just haptics.
