Vibration only

Quiet for them. Clear for you.

Most alarms go off in the room. Snoozzo stays on your wrist. At the time you set, it vibrates, and that’s it — no speaker, no ringtone, no song leaking through the wall. Your partner, roommate, or sibling can keep sleeping.

The wake check

A check that you’re actually up.

It’s easy to swipe an alarm and roll back over. Snoozzo asks you to complete a short wake check on the watch. A few minutes later, it checks again. Miss either one, and the buzzing comes back.

Your morning, your settings

Start gentle, or don’t.

A pre-alarm can build up quietly before the real thing. You can change how strong the taps feel, pick a pattern, and cap how many times you’re allowed to snooze. It’s your morning, not a default one.

On the watch

It never leaves your wrist.

Snoozzo is a watch-only app. You set alarms, change settings, and keep a wake streak right on the Apple Watch — no iPhone app to keep open, no account to create. Everything stays on the device. It works offline, and it doesn’t collect data.

A silent alarm for the person who has to get up, and the person who doesn’t.
