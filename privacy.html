Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 14, 2026

Snoozzo is a watch-only alarm app for Apple Watch. It is designed to work entirely on your device.

What the app stores

The app saves your alarms, wake settings (such as haptic intensity, pattern, snooze limits, and pre-alarm ramp), and local wake history (including streaks) on the Apple Watch. This information never leaves the device.

What the app does not collect

Snoozzo does not create an account, does not require a sign-in, and does not use iCloud, analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or any network connection. The app does not send information to Edward Dunn or to any third party.

Permissions

The app uses Apple Watch haptic and alarm/session features to deliver a vibration-only wake on your wrist. It does not access the microphone, camera, contacts, location, or Health data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated text will be posted on this page. When the snoozzo.com domain is live, the policy will also be published at https://snoozzo.com/privacy.

Contact

For questions about this policy or the app, see the support page or email support@snoozzo.com.
